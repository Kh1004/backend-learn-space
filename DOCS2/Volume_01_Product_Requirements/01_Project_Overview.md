# Project Overview

## Vision

Build a modern education platform where verified teachers publish
educational materials and students discover, read, download, discuss and
track learning progress.

## Objectives

-   Centralize educational content
-   Improve discoverability
-   Provide analytics
-   Build scalable architecture
-   Support future LMS features

## Stakeholders

-   Students
-   Teachers
-   Administrators
-   Platform Owner
